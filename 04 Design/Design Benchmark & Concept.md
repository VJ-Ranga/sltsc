# SLTSC Design Benchmark & Concept

Back to [[00 Home]] · Context: [[Positioning & Audience]] · Tokens: [[Design System]] · Structure: [[Page Wireframes]] · Mood: [[Mood & References]]

## Brief

SLTSC should feel like a calm, credible, instructor-led academy for Sri Lankan school leavers, undergraduates, career switchers and working IT staff. The experience must make courses, practical work, schedule, LKR pricing and the next step obvious. Poppins remains the required typeface; current navy/blue/aqua tokens remain the starting point. Claims, Cisco status, badges and outcomes stay verification-gated under [[Cisco Branding Rules]].

## The visual gap

The visible gap is a **local, evidence-led learning operating system**: no prominent Sri Lankan Cisco/cyber academy currently makes one learner’s route—from “I am starting” to a specific lab, batch, fee and next credential—feel like a coherent, calm visual journey. Existing sites tend to show institutional authority, a catalogue, logos or promotional claims. They rarely combine:

- a diagnostic starting point for different student levels;
- a network-shaped roadmap tied to real course prerequisites;
- proof-of-practice cards (Packet Tracer/CML/real equipment, only when true);
- local intake, LKR instalment and WhatsApp decisions in the same flow;
- instructor guidance and honest Cisco status without borrowing Cisco’s identity.

That is the territory for SLTSC: **serious enough for parents and employers, clear enough for a first-time learner, practical enough for an aspiring network or security professional.**

## Three design concepts

### 1. Signal Path — recommended

**Big idea:** learning is a network route. Each capability is a node; each course is a reliable hop; labs prove that the route works. The metaphor is useful, not decorative.

**Hero:** a clean animated topology diagram grows from a learner node to “CCNA 200-301” and branches to Network, Cybersecurity and Automation. Beside it: “Build the route to your next IT role.” Primary CTA `Find your starting point`; secondary `View next intakes`.

**Signature interaction:** a “choose your starting node” diagnostic (school leaver / some IT / working IT) highlights a recommended route and reveals prerequisites, approximate effort, mode and next batch. It can be a simple accessible stepper, not a quiz gimmick.

**Palette:** #0a1a2f base, #00aaff active path, #00ffcc completed/proof state, #f4f7fb reading sections, #f59e0b limited seats. Thin lines, no glow overload.

**Type:** Poppins 400–700 throughout; JetBrains Mono only for real commands, exam codes and small network labels.

**Why Sri Lankan students:** removes uncertainty (“where do I start?”, “what follows CCNA?”, “can I afford this?”) and turns a global certification into a visible local route with batches, fees and a teacher.

### 2. Packet Journey

**Big idea:** follow one packet from source to destination; each page section maps a technical idea to a learning outcome. The student sees how fundamentals become a working network and then a secured one.

**Hero:** a luminous but restrained packet travels across a horizontal route: device → switch → router → secured service. Copy: “Learn what happens between the click and the connection.” CTA `Explore the CCNA route`.

**Signature interaction:** scroll-driven packet journey; stopping at a hop opens the related module, lab evidence and “what you can do after this”. Reduced-motion mode becomes a static annotated diagram.

**Palette:** navy and blue foundation; aqua packet trail; white/light-blue content panels; occasional coral/orange alert state for troubleshooting. Use #00ffcc as a small signal, not a background.

**Type:** Poppins for all explanatory copy; JetBrains Mono for packet headers, IP examples and CLI snippets.

**Why Sri Lankan students:** makes an abstract subject visual and memorable for beginners, while still feeling professional to working learners. It also gives the academy a natural lab-story format.

### 3. Clear Command

**Big idea:** the interface is a clean editorial field with a small command-line accent. The message is confidence through clarity: know the command, understand the system, build the career.

**Hero:** a split hero: Yasiru’s real portrait or teaching/lab image on one side; a quiet “session transcript” on the other that resolves into three facts—`course`, `next intake`, `fee`. CTA `Talk to an instructor`; secondary `Compare courses`.

**Signature interaction:** “Ask the route” cards answer real decision questions—`I am new`, `I need weekend study`, `I want cybersecurity`, `I need a corporate cohort`—and filter the course/batch view. No fake terminal typing or simulated hacking.

**Palette:** navy #0a1a2f with larger light #f4f7fb reading surfaces; blue CTA #00aaff; aqua cursor/active rule #00ffcc; muted text #cfd8e3. Less neon, more editorial whitespace.

**Type:** Poppins is mandatory for body/headings; JetBrains Mono as a restrained metadata accent only.

**Why Sri Lankan students:** approachable and trustworthy for families and career switchers; it makes Yasiru’s human teaching role visible without turning the academy into a creator fan page.

## Recommendation: Signal Path

Choose **Signal Path**. It owns the strongest unmet territory—visible, practical progression—and connects directly to the existing [[Sitemap]], [[Course Catalog]] and [[Page Wireframes]]. It can express CCNA, cybersecurity and automation without changing the brand for each subject. It also scales from a static first release to richer interaction later, while remaining understandable in Sinhala/English and accessible without motion.

The design rule is: **every visual node must answer one learner question**—where am I, what do I learn, what will I practise, what comes next, when can I join, and what will it cost. The topology is the information architecture, not a background ornament.

## Home page specification — Signal Path

| Section | Content and purpose | Key visual/interaction | CTA / proof rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header | SLTSC wordmark, Courses, Roadmap, Schedule, Fees, Labs, About, language switch | Compact persistent header; active route indicator | `Enrol / ask a question`; no unverified badge |
| Hero: Find your route | One clear positioning line, instructor-led CCNA + cybersecurity in Sri Lanka, short subcopy | Topology from learner node to three route branches; static fallback | `Find your starting point`, `View courses` |
| Starting-point selector | New to IT / Some IT / Working IT | Three accessible cards highlight a suggested route | Recommendation must be labelled guidance, not a promise |
| Trust strip | Verified instructor credentials, real teaching/lab facts, partner or TVEC status only if confirmed | Small proof nodes connected to the route | Link to evidence or omit; never fill with placeholder numbers |
| Core route | Foundation → CCNA → Professional, with cyber and automation branches | Horizontal desktop / vertical mobile stepper; show current, next and optional nodes | `See the roadmap`; exam wording follows [[Cisco Branding Rules]] |
| Course cards | Offered courses only: level, exam code, hours, mode, fee, next intake | Cards attach to route nodes; compare/filter by goal and level | `View course`; fees are real or marked pending internally, never invented |
| Practical proof | Packet Tracer/CML/real gear images or screenshots when supplied | “What you will practise” cards: configure, troubleshoot, secure | `See labs`; captions identify tool and scenario accurately |
| Next intakes | Course, date, day/time, mode, seats, fee/installment | Clear table; mobile cards; route node pulses only for actual open intake | `Reserve / ask on WhatsApp`; seats-left data must be current |
| Instructor | Yasiru’s photo, teaching approach, verified credentials | Instructor node is the human guide through the route | `Meet Yasiru`; no unverified “12+ years/student-count” claims |
| Outcomes without hype | Skills and job directions: NOC, network support, junior security, further study | Four restrained outcome cards; avoid guaranteed employment | `See student success` only when testimonials/evidence exist |
| Corporate route | Custom team training and enquiry | Separate branch from learner route, visually quieter | `Request corporate training` |
| FAQ | Entry requirements, exam/voucher separation, mode, payments, Cisco relationship | Accessible accordion with route labels | `Ask a question`; legal/trademark copy from strategy notes |
| Final route CTA | “Ready to choose your next hop?” plus short lead form and WhatsApp | Route terminates at a contact node; no countdown | `Start with a free info call` / WhatsApp |
| Footer | Contact, course links, legal, bilingual links, Cisco disclaimer | Minimal topology rule | Only real contact details and verified links |

### First-release interaction boundary

The first release should prioritise a fast, accessible static route diagram, filterable course/intake content, clear fees and real lab/instructor evidence. Topology animation, scroll packet effects and diagnostics are enhancements; they must never hide content, require motion, or substitute for a clear enrolment path.
