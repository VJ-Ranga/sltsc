# Mood & References
Back to [[00 Home]] · Tokens in [[Design System]]

## Direction
Dark-tech, precise, calm. Motifs: **network topology** (nodes and links as subtle background lines) and **terminal** (CLI prompts, mono snippets). Not cheesy hacker clichés: no matrix rain, no green-on-black skulls, no hooded figures.

## Do
- Navy base with sparing cyan/aqua highlights
- Thin-line topology graphics (routers, switches, links) as section dividers
- Real lab photos and Packet Tracer/CML screenshots ⏳
- Mono type only where it is real CLI
- Light sections for fees/schedule to aid readability

## Don't
- Glowing neon overload, glitch text, fake "hacking" UIs
- Stock photos of people typing in hoodies
- Cisco/NetAcad logos only used if academy status is confirmed (⏳ waiting on client), following Cisco logo guidelines; otherwise text-only "exam-aligned" wording
