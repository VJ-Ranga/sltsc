## Common IA pattern

1. Institutional trust: About/heritage, leadership, approvals, partners.
2. Academic discovery: Faculties/schools → programme type or level → programme detail.
3. Conversion: intake/application, entry requirements, fees, scholarships, contact.
4. Proof: faculty, alumni/student stories, careers/industry, facilities, counters.
5. Belonging: student life, clubs, events, news, research, campuses.
6. Utility: portals, downloads, FAQ, policies, contact and location.

## How SLTSC can beat the benchmark

- Build the **online learning route** into the primary IA: How it works → programme → lab preview → schedule/fees → apply.
- Organise by **Schools + progression levels**, not a single CCNA landing page: Networking, Cybersecurity, Software/Web, then Data/Cloud/AI as future growth.
- Make every programme page decision-ready: level, audience, prerequisites, duration, live schedule, recordings, lab access, fee/payment plan, outcomes, next step.
- Make practical learning visible: browser/lab screenshots, instructor walkthroughs, projects, assessment examples, and a labelled “Demo info — to be confirmed” state for placeholders.
- Use Yasiru as a human editorial anchor, while separating founder story, faculty proof, and institutional claims.
- Add university-grade pages early—Admissions, Student Community, Events, Resources, Corporate Training—so the brand feels like a growing academy, not a course flyer.

## Summary

SLTSC should look and behave like a credible online university-in-the-making: broad enough for future schools, specific enough to help someone choose a first course today, and evidence-led about every claim.
