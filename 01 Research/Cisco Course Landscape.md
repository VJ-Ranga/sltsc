## Certification tracks
| Track | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CCST | Entry | Networking, Cybersecurity, IT Support |
| CCNA 200-301 | Associate | Flagship |
| CCNP Enterprise | Professional | Core 350-401 + one concentration exam |
| CCNP Security | Professional | Core 350-701 SCOR + concentration; 300-745 SDSI added May 2025 |
| CCNP Data Center / Service Provider / Collaboration | Professional | Likely out of scope |
| CCIE | Expert | Lab exam |
| Automation (formerly DevNet) | Associate to Expert | Renamed Feb 2026 to CCNA/CCNP/CCIE Automation (per CBT Nuggets) |

> [!warning] Verify: DevNet rename
> Source is a third party (CBT Nuggets). Confirm on cisco.com.

- Exam voucher is a separate cost from tuition.
