Overview

A structured eight-month CCNA pathway using the Cisco NetAcad curriculum across three modules. Each module earns a Cisco NetAcad Verified badge.

Who it’s for

  • Learners preparing for the Cisco CCNA (200-301) pathway
  • School leavers, undergraduates, and career switchers starting in networking
  • Working IT learners building a structured networking foundation

What you’ll gain

  • Build a foundation in network concepts and connectivity
  • Work through switching, routing, and wireless essentials
  • Understand enterprise networking, security, and automation concepts
  • Earn a Cisco NetAcad Verified badge for each completed module

Three modules, three badges

  1. 01

    Introduction to Networks (ITN)

    Explore network fundamentals, protocols, devices, media, and the foundations needed to connect and troubleshoot networks.

    Cisco NetAcad Verified badge
  2. 02

    Switching, Routing and Wireless Essentials (SRWE)

    Build practical understanding of Ethernet switching, VLANs, routing, wireless connectivity, and access-layer operation.

    Cisco NetAcad Verified badge
  3. 03

    Enterprise Networking, Security and Automation (ENSA)

    Develop enterprise routing and security concepts alongside controller-based networking and automation foundations.

    Cisco NetAcad Verified badge

Questions

Is the schedule fixed?

Delivery mode and schedule are to be confirmed with the academy before each intake.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Instalments are available for the CCNA programme. Ask the academy for the current plan.

Does enquiring guarantee a place?

No. An enquiry is not an acceptance. The academy reviews fit, timing and the next intake.
