## Personas
| Persona | Goal | Needs from site | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| School leaver / undergrad | Start an IT career, add a credential | Entry path (CCST/CCNA), fees, instalments, schedule | WhatsApp, "Book a free info call" |
| Career switcher | Move into networking/security | Roadmap, job outcomes (only true ones), prerequisites | "See the roadmap", enrol |

| Corporate HR / team lead | Train a team | Custom programs, invoicing, on-site options | "Request corporate quote" |

## Brand relationship: SLTSC vs ynh.lk
| Option | Description | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (recommended) | SLTSC = academy site; ynh.lk = Yasiru's personal brand site linking to SLTSC for enrolment | Clear roles; SEO focus on one domain; personal brand can host blog/YouTube/community | Two sites to maintain |
| B | Single site (SLTSC) with ynh.lk redirecting | Simplest | Loses personal-brand upside |
| C | Single site (ynh.lk) with SLTSC as sub-brand | Strong personality | Weakens academy as a business; harder corporate sales |

**Recommendation: A.** SLTSC handles courses, fees, enrolment. ynh.lk shows credentials, content and story, with prominent "Train with me at SLTSC" links, and SLTSC's instructor page links back. Shared design tokens ([[Design System]]) keep them consistent.
