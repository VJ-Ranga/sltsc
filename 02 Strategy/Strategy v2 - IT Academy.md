# Strategy v2 — Online IT Academy

Back to [[00 Home]] · Research: [[SL University Benchmark]] · IA: [[Sitemap v2]]

## Strategic direction

**SLTSC Academy is an online-first IT academy that helps Sri Lankan learners move from foundations to job-relevant technology capability—starting with networking and cybersecurity, and growing toward a full IT university model.**

**Brand promise:** Learn with guidance, practise with real tools, and see your next step clearly.

**Positioning:** Human, practical, and progressive. More structured than a creator course; more accessible and personal than a large campus institution.

## Audiences and jobs to be done

| Audience | Need | Primary path |
|---|---|---|
| School leaver / beginner | Understand where to start and what it costs | Foundations → certificate → live orientation |
| Career switcher | A credible, guided route into IT | Roadmap → school/programme → mentor call |
| Working IT learner | Flexible advancement and exam preparation | Professional certificate → labs → next level |
| Parent / sponsor | Safety, structure, value, proof | Admissions → fees/payment plans → FAQ |
| Corporate team / HR | Practical upskilling with clear delivery | Corporate Training → enquiry |

| School of Data, Cloud & AI | **Future school — Demo info — to be confirmed** | Data literacy, cloud, Python/data, AI foundations |

### Programme types

Short courses → Professional certificates → Diplomas → Future foundation / degree pathways. Do not call SLTSC a university, degree-awarding institution, accredited provider, or Cisco partner until legally and factually confirmed.

### Learning model

Live online classes; recordings and replay library; guided virtual labs; LMS resources and assessments; mentor/Q&A touchpoints; cohort community; Sinhala/English support where available. Any claim about 100% practical delivery, lab platform, batch size, recordings, or language must be confirmed before publication.

## Strategic guardrails

1. Never imply government, UGC, TVEC, Cisco, university, or international accreditation without documentation.
2. Never use invented testimonials, graduate outcomes, ratings, enrolment numbers, fees, or logos.
3. Keep current offerings distinct from future schools and degree pathways.
4. Make online delivery feel tangible through visuals and product detail, not generic “learn anywhere” copy.
5. Keep [[Positioning & Audience]] and [[Course Catalog]] as v1 references; this note is the v2 strategic direction.

## Summary

The v2 brand is an academy with university ambition: focused enough to win in networking and cybersecurity now, architected to add software, data, cloud, AI, foundations, and future pathways without redesigning the whole website.
