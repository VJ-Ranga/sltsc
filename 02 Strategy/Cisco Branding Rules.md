# Cisco Branding Rules
Back to [[00 Home]] · Related: [[Cisco Course Landscape]]

> [!warning] Not legal advice
> Summary of research. Confirm against Cisco's logo guide: https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/about/brand-center/logo-usage-guidelines.html

> [!success] Confirmed
> SLTSC Academy is an official Cisco Networking Academy. NetAcad wording is approved; use text and styled badge chips only until badge images are supplied.

| | Approved Cisco Networking Academy | Not an Academy |
|---|---|---|
| Say "Cisco Networking Academy" | Yes | No |
| Use NetAcad logo | Yes, intact and unaltered | No |
| Say "Cisco partner / authorized / official" | No, unless separately proven | No |
| Say "prepares you for Cisco CCNA 200-301 exam" | Yes | Yes |
| Say instructor holds a cert | Yes, if verified (Credly) | Yes, if verified |
| Promise you will pass / certify | No | No |

## Always
- The academy does not certify; exams are taken at Pearson VUE.
- Exam voucher is separate from tuition.
- Do not alter or recolor Cisco logos; do not use them in the SLTSC logo.

## Safe wording
- "CCNA 200-301 exam preparation course"
- "Prepares you for the Cisco CCNA (200-301) exam, taken at Pearson VUE"
- "Taught by a Cisco-certified instructor" (only after Credly proof)
- "Cisco, CCNA, and Cisco Networking Academy are trademarks of Cisco Systems, Inc."

## Avoid
- "Official Cisco training", "Cisco authorized partner", "Cisco Academy" (if not approved)
- "Guaranteed CCNA certification"
- Cisco logo in header/hero as if it were a partner badge
