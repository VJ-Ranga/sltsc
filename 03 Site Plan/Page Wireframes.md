## Course detail template
- Hero: course name, exam code, level, duration, mode, fee from LKR ⏳; CTA "Enrol" + "Ask on WhatsApp" (prefilled with course name)
- Sticky side card: next batches, seats left, fee, instalments, CTA
- Overview and who it is for
- What you will learn (outcomes)
- Module outline (accordion)
- Labs and tools (Packet Tracer / CML / real gear ⏳)
- Prerequisites
- Exam info: Pearson VUE, voucher separate, honest wording ([[Cisco Branding Rules]])
- Syllabus PDF download
- Instructor card
- Next steps in roadmap
- Course FAQ
- Final CTA + short lead form

## About / Instructor
- Hero: photo ⏳, name, one-line positioning
- Story and teaching approach (client copy ⏳)
- Credentials: certs + Credly badges ⏳ (verified only)
- Experience: "12+ years" only after verification
- Lab/teaching style
- Link to ynh.lk personal site ([[Positioning & Audience]])
- CTA: "Train with Yasiru" > Schedule

## Schedule & Intakes
- Filters: course, mode (weekday / weekend / online), month
- Batch table: course | start date | days/time | mode | seats left | fee | Enrol button
- Waitlist form for courses without a batch
- Note: exam voucher separate; instalment info
- CTA after table: WhatsApp

## Contact / Enrol
- Left: short lead form (name, phone, email, course, preferred mode, message), spam protection
- Right: WhatsApp button, phone, email, address + Google Map ⏳, hours ⏳, socials ⏳
- Enrol path: choose course > choose batch > submit > confirmation + WhatsApp follow-up
- CTA placement summary: header button, hero, after each course/batch table, sticky side card, final band, WhatsApp float
